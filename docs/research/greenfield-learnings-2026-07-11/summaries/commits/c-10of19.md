## Learnings for the Rebuild

### 1. **Documentation Is a First-Class Discipline**
Establish CHANGELOG.md + per-item audit-log entries (the "docs: log +NN" pattern) from day one. A 1.54:1 docs-to-code ratio initially feels high but becomes the permanent audit trail for owner recalibration. Codify in team instructions (as in CLAUDE.md § owner ask 2026-06-13).

### 2. **Slice Iteratively, Validate Before Escalating**
Snippets, palette operators, and plain-text commands all landed in 4–5 slices over days, model-first + host-testable. When pool exhaustion looms, explicitly document HOLD + escalate to owner with "why" rather than proceeding blind into risky territory. The HOLD-declare pattern (3+ instances over 2 days) is healthy restraint, not failure.

### 3. **Perf Audits Drive Targeted Fixes, Not Blind Refactoring**
TSK-838–845 shows a research phase (audit commits), then 8–10 specific, measurable wins (grammar-warm, search parallelize, file-read defer, getenv fastpath, highlight wire). Each landing is single-commit, validated, checked off. Avoid multi-day perf refactors; instead, measure + fix specific bottlenecks.

### 4. **VM Infrastructure Gating Is Non-Optional**
TSK-822 (T5 pixel-test tier gating) is small (19 lines) but critical: it closes the "host tests pass but pixels are wrong" false-green trap by self-skipping outside the GUI lane. Establish VM prerequisites (test-in-vm.sh, screencapture, MDV_VM_GUI_SESSION gate) at launch; don't retrofit.

### 5. **Supply-Chain Discipline Compounds**
TSK-783 (--locked Cargo.lock) + TSK-575 (cast deny-ratchet) are small (~30 LOC each) but establish permanent gates. A lint ratchet or a lock enforcement that fails the gate on the first violation prevents silent debt accumulation. Do these upfront in the Rust/build bootstrap.

### 6. **Handoff/Checkpoint Commits Are Load-Bearing**
Three major checkpoints (752f2c4a, 2b41c5fb, 18720dce) in 2 days consolidate lessons, prune HANDOFF.md, curate backlog, refresh board state. These are not "luxury" documentation; they are how you stay coherent across agent context compactions. Budget for them per 20–30 feature commits or per 2–3 agent swaps.
